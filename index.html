MathematicsA problem open since 1940, attacked at the radius where it first bites

The Kaplansky unit conjecture fell in 2021 when Gardam produced a counterexample over F_2. Over the integers — Higman's original question — it is still open. Radius three is closed by known results and the known counterexamples begin at radius four, so radius four is where an integral statement must first differ from a statement over a field. My programme is a study of that radius.

  • Counted the whole census. Exactly 52 nontrivial units of F_2[P] are supported in the radius-four ball — and from that, no unit of Z[P] supported in that ball can have nontrivial reduction mod 2, with no bound whatsoever on the coefficients.
  • Measured how far congruence methods reach, as an increment tower on the radius of the correction, and reformulated it in closed form so that entire congruence strata are decided at once over Z_2.
  • Proved a limit. Where the mod-2 residue is trivial, no condition on u mod 2^K that is necessary for unit-hood can decide the question — at any radius.
  • Answered a problem of Bartholdi affirmatively in the twisted-unitary sector, and proved the structure around the witness: a certified obstruction closing radius four, its completeness among σ*-invariant linear obstructions, and a witness mod 8. A compactness theorem then reduces the sector to a single certified lower bound.
  • The characteristic was never the obstruction. Over any field containing a primitive 8th root of unity, all 52 census classes carry exactly sixteen units; over Q and R, none — certified 52/52. Gardam's sixteen complex units form a single gauge orbit, and the real obstruction is ζ₈.
The bundle verifies itself. Extract the ancillary archive into an empty directory, run python3 src/flagship_numbers_gate.py, and the paper's headline numbers are recomputed from the shipped files alone — including a determinant-criterion check that the 52 census supports really are units. 126 scripts, 415 result files, 9 certificates.

CryptographySQIsign, every arithmetic layer, one falsifiable thesis

Address- and control-flow constant-time can be achieved and verified at every layer — yet the operand channel remains open at every layer.

Ten single-author manuscripts spanning the quaternion-layer Cornacchia routine, the field multiplier, and theta-coordinate normalisation. Measured, not asserted.

  • CA-BPN. Affine normalisation for theta-coordinate (2,2)-isogeny chains, formulated as a scheduling problem and solved with a deterministic cache-budgeted batch-size policy. I stated and proved the correctness conditions for delayed normalisation, implemented a constant-time Rust template with fixed loop bounds and uniform memory access, and benchmarked it on Apple M2. Reproduction harness is public.
  • Countermeasure costs, measured on the reference implementation. Exponent blinding (+25%) is ineffective in the model. Per-step re-randomisation (~3.1× overhead) is the only model-validated defence. The reference-free collision setting is isolated as an open problem rather than papered over.
  • A protocol designed to be able to fail. Stages A–D make the operand-channel thesis falsifiable on hardware, ending in an uncomfortable prediction: trace-equality holds on the board while the power trace still carries operands.
  • New ground: threshold and distributed SQIsign (three barriers that make group-action techniques non-transferable), and verifier-side fault hardening for IoT — the side that actually receives attacker-controlled input.
  • Bridging back to mathematics: certified, explicit spectral-gap bounds for the genus-2 superspecial (2,2)-isogeny graph, at the exact parameters SQIsign-family schemes use.

Core Cornacchia findings were assessed as sound in a technical call with the TU Graz SQIsign group (S. Sinha Roy). The field-multiplier manuscript was revised following detailed review by Ł. Chmielewski (Masaryk University). I am currently seeking a laboratory partner for the hardware-measurement stage.

MethodFalsification first

I run adversarial audits against my own results before anything is circulated. Two of my own conjectures were refuted and retired this way. What the audits actually catch is instructive:

  • Reading a path is not running it. I deleted a seed file no claim referenced — and the generator that consumed it silently lost its input. The chain of reasoning was intact; the execution path was dead.
  • Gates check citations, not needs. A generator no theorem names is never flagged, so a naive gate stays green forever. Fixed by scanning backwards, from shipped artifacts to the procedures that produce them.
  • Fixes introduce new errors. A sentence repaired during an audit turned out to be false itself. Replacement text now gets re-audited at the same strength.
  • Selection effects. A set chosen by extremising a statistic cannot be validated by a function of that same statistic. You have to change the instrument.

Each finding is converted into an invariant rather than a one-off patch. Statuses are reported exactly — published, under review, in preparation — and negative results are disclosed next to positive ones.
